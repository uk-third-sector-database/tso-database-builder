\section{Beta release 3: September 30 2024}

Third public release. Now includes data from Companies House bulk download released September 2024.
Bug fix to ensure dissolution dates are in the main spine except in the case where at least one of the 
matched organisations have not been removed from the relevant register. In all other cases a removed entity should
now have a removal date in the main spine. If there were competing dates (as in the case of a linked organisation), the most recent
date will be in the main spine, with alternatives in the supplementary table.

\section{Beta release 4: December 10 2024}
Fourth public release. Edits to algorithm to improve the presentation of matches. In particular, matches from within the saem source, such as charities which reregister, have the most recently 
active organisation present on the main spine. Previously the removal date was not being used to sort matches.
